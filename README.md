## Required Google Sheets

### Sessions
Stores session state and peer-review configuration.

Columns:

- session_id
- instructor_id
- phase
- created_at
- archived
- is_open
- is_active
- active_until
- peer_open
- peer_opened_at
- peer_required_reviews

---

### Responses
Stores student submissions.

Columns:

- response_id
- session_id
- reviewer_token
- timestamp
- response_text_original
- response_text_edited
- confidence
- instructor_tags
- instructor_feedback
- is_hidden

---

### PeerAssignments
Stores peer-review allocations.

Columns:

- session_id
- reviewer_token
- response_id
- assigned_at

---

### PeerFeedback
Stores submitted peer reviews.

Columns:

- feedback_id
- session_id
- response_id
- reviewer_token
- feedback_text
- timestamp
- review_quality
- archived

---

### Instructors
Stores instructor login credentials.

Columns:

- instructor_id
- instructor_name
- password

---

### Profanity
List of words to be filtered from displayed responses.

Columns:

- term

---

### Notes

- `session_id` links all activity to a specific teaching session.
- `reviewer_token` is an anonymous student identifier generated by the application.
- Responses, peer reviews, and assignments are all tied to a session through `session_id`.
- Peer review assignments are generated on demand when students enter review mode.
